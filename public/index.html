How to play

  • Everyone starts with the same cash. Roll the dice on your turn and move around the board.
  • Doubles give you another roll — three in a row send you to prison.
  • Buy the cities, airports and companies you land on. Other players pay you rent when they stop there.
  • Own every city of a country to build houses and hotels and multiply the rent.
  • Trade with friends, mortgage when cash is tight, and be the last one standing.
